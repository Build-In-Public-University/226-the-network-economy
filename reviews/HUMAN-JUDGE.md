# Human judge worksheet

Status: pending human adjudication.

The pro and con reviews are independent cases. Neither is a verdict. The human judge should score the claim after reading both cases and inspecting the source-linked ledger.

Suggested verdict vocabulary:

- `supported_by_source` — the SEP directly supports the ancestry or mechanism claim;
- `plausible_extension` — the proposed framework adds a coherent, testable layer;
- `underdefined` — the claim cannot yet be evaluated because key terms or boundaries are missing;
- `redundant_until_residual` — existing concepts may already cover it;
- `falsified` — a specified test or counterexample defeats the claim;
- `open` — not enough evidence either way.

Suggested confidence: `low`, `medium`, or `high`.

## Judge's minimum evidence gate

Do not mark the network economy `validated` unless a preregistered, held-out comparison shows incremental value against:

1. money-only accounting;
2. labor-time accounting;
3. conventional externality/life-cycle accounting;
4. a hybrid or polycentric governance baseline.

The comparison must measure both benefit and cost:

- delayed-burden prediction;
- burden-allocation accuracy;
- future option-set preservation;
- reversibility;
- affected-party control;
- administrative labor;
- privacy/surveillance cost;
- robustness under time, node, and boundary shuffling.

## Pro/con convergence

Both reviewers agree on the following:

- The SEP provides substantial mechanistic ancestry.
- The proposed framework is strongest as a measurement and falsification program.
- “Time violence” needs either a residual class or measurable improvement over existing concepts.
- The network model must be compared against money-only, labor-time, conventional externality, and hybrid/polycentric baselines.
- Future weighting should remain conditional until uncertainty, population, governance, and present-need conflicts are specified.
- A network ledger can reproduce planner, surveillance, false-precision, and administrative-domination failures.
- None of this currently proves that capitalism is necessarily oppressive.

That convergence is not proof. It is agreement about the next test. Very efficient. Almost suspicious.
